Crop Model Analysis

The crop recommendation model learns from 2,200 labelled examples covering 22 crops, with 100 examples per crop.

Inputs
  • N, P, K: nitrogen, phosphorus and potassium content of the soil
  • Temperature (°C) and humidity (%)
  • pH of the soil
  • Rainfall (mm)
Method
  1. Clean and inspect the data, then split it 80% training / 20% testing.
  2. Train and compare an MLP classifier, a bagging classifier and a random forest.
  3. Save the best model and use it to serve predictions in this app.

See notebooks/crop_recommendation_model.ipynb in the repository for the full training workflow.
