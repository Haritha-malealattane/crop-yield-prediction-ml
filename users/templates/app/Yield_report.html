Yield Model Analysis

Yield is the amount of agricultural product harvested per unit of land, and it is a key measure of farm productivity and profitability.

Inputs

  • Area and its units
  • Production and its units
Output

A predicted yield value, shown together with a plot of the entered values. Each prediction is saved and listed on the Yield DB page.
