AI-Powered Crop Yield Prediction

Agriculture depends on decisions made under uncertainty. This project uses machine learning to support two of them: choosing a crop and estimating its yield.

  • Crop prediction: soil and weather inputs → recommended crop.
  • Yield prediction: crop, season, area and production → expected yield.
  • Every prediction is saved so it can be reviewed later.
